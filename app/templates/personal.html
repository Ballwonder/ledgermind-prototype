Financial profiles

Profiles control accounting and tax treatment. The payment account and the economic owner are separate concepts.

Create profile

The profile type determines the rule regime used downstream.

Treatment boundaries

The profile now changes the actual accounting decision engine—not just labels or filters.
Personal
Cash flow, categories, goals, net worth. No bookkeeping/tax deduction engine by default.
Sole proprietor
Business accounting, mixed-use allocations, T2125-oriented tax treatment, GST/HST context.
Corporation
Corporate bookkeeping, T2/GIFI context, shareholder/employee treatment when personal use is involved.
Rental property
Property-specific income/expenses, repairs vs improvements, T776-oriented treatment.
